# Current Site Audit — The Gas Man

Audit date: August 27, 2026. Sources were limited to public pages on [thegasmanfuel.com](https://www.thegasmanfuel.com/), its linked Jotform forms, and indexed public search results. No private customer information was collected.

## What exists today

The current site has a simple public navigation: Home, Memberships, Pit Stop, Boat Fill-Ups, Fleet, Emergency Fuel, About, FAQ, and Terms. Most pages use direct Call Now and Email Us actions. Membership, Pit Stop, Emergency Fuel, boat, and fleet actions leave the website for Jotform.

Verified business messaging:

- Local, family-run mobile fuel delivery with the tagline “Stay Fueled. Stay Moving.”
- Published service area: Columbia, Blythewood, Elgin, Lugoff, Camden, and nearby areas.
- Fuel types: Regular Unleaded, Premium, and Diesel.
- Residential/member delivery, one-time Pit Stop delivery, emergency fuel requests, fleet fueling, and boat fill-ups.
- Phone: (803) 461-1414. Email: info@thegasmanfuel.com.
- Hours: Monday–Friday 7 AM–7 PM; Saturday 8 AM–12 PM; Sunday closed.
- Membership benefit: $0.10 off per gallon. Fuel is billed separately.
- Cruise Control: $30/month, one vehicle, up to four fill-ups per month.
- Fast Lane: $40/month, two vehicles, up to six fill-ups per month.
- Optional $10 add-on for a five-gallon gas-can top-off.
- Pit Stop currently publishes a $15 service fee plus delivered fuel.
- Membership terms say monthly billing occurs on the enrollment date, with recurring processing through Authorize.Net.
- Published social accounts: Facebook profile id 61580913907189 and Instagram @thegasmanfuel.

No public physical street address, named owners, real customer testimonials, star rating, certifications, license numbers, or exact coverage boundary were found. Those items remain intentionally absent from the demo.

## Current customer journey

The public site explains the service, then sends customers into separate Jotform flows. The membership form collects customer contact details, plan, card data, billing address, service address, vehicle color and tag, fuel type, referral, notes, and agreement to terms. The form displays Authorize.Net-backed recurring billing.

This produces a fragmented flow: website → hosted form → payment processor → later record visibility. The reported operational issue is that a new signup may not be recognized as an active member until later reconciliation.

## Strengths

- The core “skip the pump” value proposition is easy to understand.
- Services, fuel types, areas, and contact information are published.
- Membership terms and prices are publicly available.
- Fleet and boat offerings have clear request paths.

## Improvement opportunities

- Keep enrollment, service requests, payment status, and customer records in one branded experience.
- Replace delayed reconciliation with verified, event-driven activation.
- Give staff a searchable, status-aware operations dashboard.
- Give members a confirmation page and portal with a human-readable member number.
- Improve information architecture, mobile conversion paths, accessibility, SEO metadata, analytics events, and service-area content.
- Clarify conflicting failed-payment terms: the public Terms page says one business day and up to two attempts, while the membership form describes a five-day grace period and ten-day cancellation window. Owner/legal review is required.
- Confirm whether boat service is fully launched everywhere: FAQ copy and the dedicated boat page have shown different timing language in indexed versions.

## Audit limitations

The root domain intermittently returned an automated fetch error, but indexed www pages and public linked forms were reachable. The audit did not submit forms, process payments, scrape customer data, or verify internal Jotform/Authorize.Net settings. Performance, accessibility, and analytics scripts were not exhaustively measured.
